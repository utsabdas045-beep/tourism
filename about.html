About Tripura

Tripura is one of India’s northeastern states, bordered by Bangladesh on three sides. Though small in size, it is rich in natural beauty, biodiversity, and cultural heritage.

The state was ruled for centuries by the Manikya dynasty, whose legacy is reflected in historic landmarks such as Ujjayanta Palace and Neermahal.

Tripura’s identity is deeply rooted in its tribal communities, traditional dances like Hojagiri, colorful festivals, and bamboo handicrafts, making it a culturally vibrant destination.
